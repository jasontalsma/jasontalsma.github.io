The goal of this project was to create the remote microphone and infrared receiver to be used in concert with the Mirror product. I was the lead and sole engineer for all mechanical tasks from pre-conception to first shipments.

As the design lead, I spearheaded the entire product design, testing, and verification I coordinated with electrical and manufacturing teams to navigate the space constraints of the device, optimize IR, improve audio, and perfect button feel. With each iteration, I balanced aesthetic, functional priorities, and shifting constraints.

![](images/mirror-teacher-reflecting.png)

A consistent dialogue with production teams in China and Thailand kept the project on track. I interfaced directly with factory engineers (remotely and boots on the ground), ensuring design intent through tooling, fixture design, modifications, and production.

The final design included a plastic remote shell equipped with IR receivers, high fidelity microphone, LED feedback, 5-button input, internal antennae, and a creative bolo-tie style lanyard system for wearing the device.

The lanyard design, a major pain point in previous versions, underwent countless iterations and re-explorations largely based on user testing.

The docking and charging aspects of the product demanded precision and fine-tuning. Every interaction point had to be seamless and intuitive, which meant extensive user testing and engineering validation. Meanwhile, the team’s parallel design strategies resulted in continuous spec updates, requiring numerous tool mods. Agility was key for success.

Material selection was a critical task when it came to ensuring hidden LED transparency with a functioning IR system. Testing and verification were executed with my direct input or oversight across all phases.

![](images/pxl_20220419_182529163.mp_.jpg)

## Major Challenges

One of the toughest challenges was engineering snap features inside an extremely tight cavity. This required innovative thinking and close collaboration with factory engineers to solve a puzzle of lifts and slides in the tiniest space. Careful planning and constant back-and-forth between all teams and companies involved, was key to delivering a reliable product.

![](images/pxl_20231101_221956072.jpg)

We encountered a mysterious buzzing issue from the microphone, which led to weeks of intensive testing. Through process of elimination, I traced the problem back to a misfit in the rubber sealant around the microphone stack. In future designs, I would opt for a thru-mounted microphone on the PCB no question.
